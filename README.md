# 👟 Adidas Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Adidas sales data using Microsoft Excel to uncover meaningful business insights into sales performance, product categories, retailer contributions, regional performance, and profitability.

The project transforms raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, evaluate business performance, and make data-driven decisions.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales revenue, units sold, operating profit, operating margin, and sales trends across different business dimensions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Analyse overall sales performance and profitability.
- Identify the highest-performing retailers by sales revenue.
- Evaluate product category performance.
- Compare sales and operating profit across geographical regions.
- Examine monthly sales trends and identify peak sales periods.
- Compare operating profit across different sales methods.
- Develop actionable business recommendations based on the findings.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from product sales. |
| Total Units Sold | Total quantity of products sold. |
| Total Operating Profit | Operating profit generated from sales activities. |
| Operating Margin | Operating profit expressed as a percentage of sales revenue. |

### Operating Margin Formula

Operating margin measures the proportion of sales revenue retained as operating profit.

\[
\text{Operating Margin} = \frac{\text{Operating Profit}}{\text{Total Sales}} \times 100
\]

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis produced the following findings:

### 1. Retailer Performance
- West Gear was the leading retailer by sales, generating approximately **$242.96 million**.
- Foot Locker ranked second, with approximately $220.09 million in sales.
- Walmart and Amazon recorded comparatively lower sales among the retailers displayed.

### 2. Product Category Performance
- Men's Street Footwear was the leading product category by sales, generating approximately **$208.83 million**.
- Women's Apparel followed with approximately $179.04 million.
- Women's Athletic Footwear recorded the lowest sales among the six product categories displayed, at approximately $106.63 million.

### 3. Regional Performance
- The West was the strongest region by sales, generating approximately **$269.94 million**.
- It also recorded the highest operating profit, at approximately $89.61 million.
- The Midwest recorded the lowest sales among the five regions displayed, at approximately $135.80 million.

### 4. Monthly Sales Trends
- July recorded the highest monthly sales, at approximately **$95 million**.
- August followed with approximately $92 million in sales.
- Sales declined after August, reaching approximately $64 million in October before recovering towards the end of the year.

### 5. Overall Profitability
The dashboard displayed the following headline figures:

- **Total Sales:** Approximately $899.90 million.
- **Total Operating Profit:** Approximately $332.13 million.
- **Operating Margin:** 42.30%.
- **Total Units Sold:** Approximately 2.48 million.

These figures should be interpreted according to the definitions and calculations used in the underlying dataset.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Improve Retailer Performance:** Investigate the factors contributing to comparatively lower sales at Walmart and Amazon. Review product availability, pricing, promotions, and retailer-specific sales trends.

2. **Strengthen Men's Street Footwear Performance:** Investigate the drivers of strong sales and evaluate whether inventory or production increases are justified by demand forecasts, stock levels, and profitability.

3. **Investigate Women's Athletic Footwear:** Examine product demand, pricing, inventory availability, and customer preferences to understand its comparatively lower sales.

4. **Leverage Regional Success:** Study the West region's product mix, retailer contributions, and sales methods to identify strategies that could be adapted to other regions.

5. **Optimise Inventory Planning:** Use historical monthly sales patterns to support demand forecasting and prepare for peak sales periods.

6. **Monitor Profitability:** Evaluate operating profit and operating margin alongside revenue to ensure that business growth translates into sustainable financial performance.
